# Red Hat × Intel Brand System

- Preserve the Red Hat-first lockup: Red Hat logo, multiplication sign, Intel logo.
- Use locally bundled approved logo files; do not redraw, recolor, stretch, or crop logos.
- Use Red Hat Display for headlines, Red Hat Text for interface/body copy, and Red Hat Mono for metrics and technical values.
- Red Hat red (`#ee0000`) anchors urgency and primary identity. Intel cyan (`#00aeef`) marks technology, proof, and interactive emphasis.
- Use the dark stage (`#151515`) with high-contrast white text. Avoid decorative gradients that compete with evidence.
- Maintain generous negative space, one dominant idea per scene, and a maximum readable line length near 760px.
- Motion should reveal causality and hierarchy. Default transitions are 400ms or less and must honor reduced-motion preferences.
