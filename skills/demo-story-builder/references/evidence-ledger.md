# Evidence Ledger

Classify every material claim before turning it into a scene.

- `verified-fact`: supported by an approved source or stable project artifact.
- `live-measurement`: produced by the configured live system during the demo.
- `rehearsal-fixture`: representative checked-in data, visibly labeled when shown.
- `future-state`: intended or designed behavior that is not yet proven.
- `interpretation`: narrative conclusion derived from identified evidence.

For each claim record its owner, source, last verification date, and presentation treatment. Unsupported numerical claims do not belong in metric, comparison, or payoff scenes. Future-state claims must use future language. A rehearsal fixture demonstrates the presentation flow, not production performance.

Live-proof scenes should connect the visible result to one or more ledger entries. When a claim has no acceptable evidence, either remove it, restate it as a question, or explicitly make it a future-state statement.
